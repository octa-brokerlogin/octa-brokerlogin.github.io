Copy trading: what to understand

Review performance

Past performance is not a guarantee of future results. Look at drawdowns, volatility, trading history, risk metrics and how long a strategy has operated.

Know the costs

Copy services may involve spreads, markups or trader-specific fees. Read the applicable terms and fee details before subscribing.

Stay in control

Understand how to adjust allocations, close copied positions, stop copying and access your funds. Copying can magnify losses as well as gains.
